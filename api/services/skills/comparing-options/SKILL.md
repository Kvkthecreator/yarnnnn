# Comparing options

## Steps

1. Fix the question and the criteria with the member before gathering evidence. Criteria are what the decision turns on: five or fewer.
2. Evidence per option per criterion, cited by path or URL. A cell with no evidence says so.
3. The table: criteria as rows, options as columns, one cited fact per cell.
4. Recommend one option and state the assumptions the recommendation depends on. If the evidence does not support a choice, say that and name what evidence would.
5. Land it in the meaning-folder of the decision, with `derived_from` naming every source read.

## Quality bar

- The same criteria applied to every option.
- Evidence, not adjectives. The losing options' strengths stated.
- The recommendation follows from the table, visibly.

## Anti-patterns

Criteria chosen after the answer. A table without citations. A recommendation that ignores its own table. Comparing on what was easy to find.
