**Date**: 2026-07-29
**Status**: Proposal, built **on** the shipped slide-1 hero rather than competing with it.
**Supersedes**: `ONELINER-ICP-GTM-PROPOSAL-2026-07-29.md` §2 (the product-sentence proposal). Everything else in that document stands and is derived from here instead.

**The canon, as shipped on slide 1:**

> # your true AI-first workspace.
> # co-work like never before.
>
> Work with multi-LLMs like chatgpt, gemini, claude together on one shared workspace.
> Dedicated apps, file system to share, work on documents with AI like never before.

---

## 1. Where I veered — conceded and held

### 1.1 — CONCEDED: "co-work". I was wrong.

I rejected it on the Anthropic Cowork collision. Re-examining:

- **You use it as a verb, not a noun.** *"co-work like never before"* is generic English. Anthropic ships a product *called* Cowork; they cannot own the verb. The collision I feared applies to `yarnnn Cowork`, not to `co-work` as an act.
- **Adjacency is an asset here, not a liability.** Cowork validates the category and does it single-vendor. *"Co-work, but not locked to one model"* is a sharper position than any category word I could invent — and it is the one claim they structurally cannot answer.
- **You already say it unprompted.** It was in your project description before it was on the slide. That is two independent instances of the same instinct, which is stronger evidence than my one structural objection.

**Ruling: co-work is canon.** My §2 alternate is withdrawn.

### 1.2 — CONCEDED: capability-led, not ownership-led

I led with *"the workspace you own."* You led with *"true AI-first"* and demoted ownership to the possessive *"your."*

You are right, and my own §7 said so before I saw your slide: *"nobody wakes up wanting ownership; they want it retroactively, after losing something."* ADR-486's consumption guard is the same warning — *"Era-1's failure was accumulation nobody felt."* An ownership-led hero walks straight into it. Yours doesn't.

**Ruling: capability forward, ownership as the possessive and the proof.** With one cost, in §3.

### 1.3 — CONVERGED, independently: the multi-LLM thesis

Your subhead: *"Work with multi-LLMs like chatgpt, gemini, claude together on one shared workspace."*

My §1: *"the second principal is not necessarily a person — it is most often a second surface."*

Same claim, arrived at from opposite directions — you from the product, me from ADR-445's principal roles and ADR-460's hands ruling. **This is the strongest signal in the whole exchange**, and it means the ICP work in the prior proposal is derived from your hero rather than imposed on it.

### 1.4 — CONCEDED: "dedicated apps, file system to share" is more concrete than my hero

I named no surfaces because the roster churns (Radar in, Images out, 2026-07-28). You named the *categories* — apps, filesystem, documents — which is the right altitude: concrete enough to be real, abstract enough to survive a Dock reshuffle. Keep it, and see §4.7 for the one discipline it requires.

### 1.5 — HELD: the mechanism is missing from the hero field

This is my one remaining objection, and it is the only place I'd ask you to change anything.

Your own discipline (GTM v4 §1, SITE-COPY-SPEC §0, ESSENCE): *capability adjectives never appear without their mechanism.* **"AI-first" is a capability adjective.** As written, the hero field makes three claims — AI-first, co-work, multi-LLM — and carries no mechanism. Notion, Slack, and Cowork can all write that headline tomorrow, and two of them have bigger megaphones.

The mechanism is *signed / traceable*, and it is the only thing none of them can copy. It should be in the same visual field as the claim, not two chapters later on slide 9.

### 1.6 — MINOR: "like never before" appears twice

Headline and subhead. It is a bare intensifier doing no work, and repeating it halves what little it does. Cut it from the subhead; keep it in the headline where it carries the rhythm.

## 2. The proposed amendment — minimal, structure untouched

**Headline: unchanged.**

> your true AI-first workspace.
> co-work like never before.

**Subhead: tightened, mechanism landed.**

> Work with ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude together in one shared workspace.
> Dedicated apps, a shared file system, documents you build with AI —
> and every change signed by whoever made it, human or not.

Three edits: proper-cased the model names (they are brands and reading them lowercase costs recognition), cut the duplicate intensifier, and added the signed clause. The last line is the whole moat in eleven words, and *"human or not"* does double duty — it lands attribution and it says co-work is species-blind, which is the thing that makes "AI-first" true rather than decorative.

## 3. What adopting your hero costs, stated plainly

Ownership is now carried by one word — *"your"* — and the moat is not in the hero at all. That is the right trade, but it has a consequence you should price deliberately:

> **Slide 9 becomes the single most load-bearing asset in the deck.** It is now the *only* place the moat is felt.

*"Composed from — you · Aug 2 / your agent · Aug 5 / Claude · Aug 6."* If the hero sells the capability, slide 9 has to close the proof, and it should be promoted from a chapter-two detail to a chapter-one payoff — immediately after the problem, not buried in the product tour.

## 4. Second-order implications, derived

### 4.1 — The ICP: unchanged, and now derived rather than asserted

**Plural · Consequential · Continuing** survives intact, and *plural* is now read directly off your own subhead. Naming three LLMs in the hero *is* the qualifier — the copy self-selects before anyone clicks.

One correction the hero forces: your framing is **lighter on consequential and continuing** than mine was. It reads AI-power-user rather than bearer-of-consequence. That is a real shift and it is the right one — it matches the ADR-490 price shape (free at two heads, thin PAYG margin, volume-dependent) far better than my wince-filtered version did. A high-usage prosumer is a better fit for this price list than a scarce $500/mo operator.

**Anti-ICP unchanged and now sharper**: the single-AI user. Your hero lists three LLMs by name; someone who uses one reads that headline and correctly concludes it isn't for them. That is the funnel doing its own qualification.

### 4.2 — The competitive frame gains a third camp

The deck names two: model makers (OpenAI/Google/Anthropic) and agent memory (Mem0/Zep/Cognee). *"True AI-first"* is not aimed at either — it is aimed at a third:

> **Camp three: AI-retrofitted workspaces.** Notion, Slack, Google Workspace. Products built for humans with AI bolted on afterward.

*"True"* is doing real work in that headline and it should be made explicit rather than left as tone. The claim is: **their AI is a feature inside a human tool; here, AI is a principal in the workspace.** That is falsifiable, defensible, and it is what slide 16 should say. Right now slide 16 lists *"Notion · Slack"* in a column without naming what makes them a different kind of competitor.

### 4.3 — The activation loop gets its real name

If co-work is the promise, the activation event is **the first co-work moment**: two distinct principals touching the same material.

That reframes the wedge from the prior proposal without changing it:

```
MCP door        →   the desk      →   the share link
first co-work       daily co-work     co-work with a person
(free, day one)     (free)            (paid at head 3)
```

The MCP door stays the acquisition wedge — and now it has an honest name in the product's own vocabulary rather than an infrastructure one. *"Co-work with the AI you already use"* is the connector-directory pitch, and it needs no explanation of what MCP is.

### 4.4 — The metric sharpens

**Time-to-first-co-work-moment**, defined as: two distinct principals with attributed revisions on the same file.

Better than the prior *time-to-second-principal* on three counts — it is stated in product vocabulary, it is measurable from the existing ledger with no new telemetry, and it counts the thing that actually matters (shared material, not merely a second grant row).

### 4.5 — The motion is unchanged and now better supported

Free-to-value, expand by head, two legs (seats + usage). Prosumer/small-team shape. The hero's AI-power-user register fits this materially better than an ownership-led, consequence-filtered hero would have — it invites volume, and volume is what a 30% PAYG margin needs.

### 4.6 — The recognition sentence still works, and gets better

> **"I use three different AIs, and I'm the only thing connecting them."**

Against your hero this reads as the setup to which *"co-work like never before"* is the punchline: you stop being the connective tissue. Keep it.

### 4.7 — The one discipline "dedicated apps" requires

Categories in the hero, never the roster. *Apps · file system · documents* is durable. *Chat · Studio · Files · Radar · Images* is a fortnight's shelf life — ADR-486's canonical desk sentence names Images, which ADR-488 hid from the Dock four days later.

Practical rule: **the app roster may appear in the product chapter and never in the hero, the subhead, or the site's above-the-fold.**

### 4.8 — Vocabulary, updated

**Add to live words**: *co-work* (verb) · *AI-first* (adjective, always with mechanism) · *signed* (the layman word for attribution) · *shared workspace*.

**"Co-work" usage rule**: verb and adjective only — *co-work with*, *a co-work moment*, *co-working over the same file*. Never a product noun, never capitalised, never `yarnnn Cowork`. That is the line that keeps the adjacency an asset instead of a collision.

**Retirements from the prior proposal are unaffected**: standing delegate · the judgment seat · delegation dial · persona-as-entity-class · five domain experts · the trust dial is the pricing axis · "operator" in external copy.

## 5. What to watch

1. **"AI-first" is a claim with a short half-life.** Every workspace product will say it within a year. What survives is *true* — and *true* is only defensible if the mechanism is visible. This is why §1.5 is the one thing I'd hold out for.
2. **The hero promises co-work; the product must deliver a co-work moment fast.** If a new user's first session is solo — one human, one desk, no second principal — the headline wrote a cheque the session didn't cash. That is the §4.4 metric's real job.
3. **"Multi-LLM" invites a feature race you have already refused.** ADR-420 §10: *"yarnnn provides enough engines… never the most (that is a marketplace, a red ocean, and off-moat)."* Naming three models in the hero is fine; a model-count comparison table is the treadmill. Hold the line.
4. **The prior proposal's kernel dependency is unchanged and now more acute.** If `foreign-llm`/`a2a` ever collapse into member attribution the way ADR-460 collapsed kernel Agents into *the member's hands*, then *"work with chatgpt, gemini, claude together"* becomes one principal wearing three faces — and the hero's central claim loses its ledger backing. This needs to be an invariant, not an implementation detail.
