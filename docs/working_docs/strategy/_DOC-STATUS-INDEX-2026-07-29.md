## Why this folder went stale, and the proposed fix

Strategy kept being *decided* — at ADR velocity, with ADR rigour — it just stopped being *recorded*
where the external story lives. The proposed structural fix (GTM-RECUT-PROPOSAL §5.8): **a
GTM-consequence line on the ADR template** for any ADR touching Identity (Axiom 2), Purpose
(Axiom 3), or Channel (Axiom 6) — one line, stated at the moment of decision, the way `Amends:` is.
ADR-445 already does this voluntarily; ADR-490 does it by delegation. Making it structural is the
difference between this pass being a fix and being a recurring chore.
